The people behind Deep Roots

Care with a whole lot of heart behind it.

Deep Roots is a locally owned and operated massage studio built around experienced therapists, personalized sessions and the belief that feeling better should feel human from the moment you walk in.

“Deep Roots” began with a family legacy of healing touch — and grew into a studio built to help people feel more at home in their bodies.

Who we are

Locally rooted. Globally informed.

Deep Roots is home to a team of passionate, highly experienced therapists whose training spans Colorado, Hawaii, Thailand, Laos, Nepal and beyond. Across different specialties and approaches, the common thread is simple: listen first, personalize the work, and make each session genuinely useful to the person on the table.

Owner Donna’s connection to massage started long before Deep Roots opened. Her grandmother, Donnagene, graduated from massage school the same year Donna was born, introducing her early to the transformative power of bodywork. The studio’s name is a tribute to that foundation — the people, practices and experiences that continue to shape the care offered here.

The goal is high-quality massage at a fair price, exceptional care from start to finish, and a studio that feels equally welcoming to Vail locals and visiting guests.

Donna

Donna blends intuition, compassion and training from around the world into sessions designed to create healing, balance and comfort in body and mind.

Donna’s story

Intentional, loving touch has been part of Donna’s life from the beginning. Her grandmother introduced her to bodywork early, and Deep Roots is a tribute to that foundation. Donna trained at the Pacific Center for Awareness and Bodywork in Kauai, studying postural alignment, deep tissue, sports massage and Swedish relaxation. She later studied Thai yoga massage in Thailand and Laos and traveled to Nepal with a group of massage therapists to host clinics and teach local bodywork. Cupping therapy is another important part of her practice.

Lydia

Lydia approaches massage as a way to come home to the body, creating space to slow down, reconnect and listen to what the body is asking for.

Lydia’s story

After a profound yoga teacher training in Baja, Lydia found her calling in body-based healing. She brings more than 14 years of experience supporting thousands of clients, 12 years as a Thai Massage and Deep Tissue instructor, certification in lymphatic drainage, training in Craniosacral Therapy through the Upledger Institute and Visceral Manipulation through the Barral Institute, plus more than 3,500 hours of massage education. Her work integrates these modalities with an emphasis on listening, safety and nervous-system support.
