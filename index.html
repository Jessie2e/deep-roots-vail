Before your visit

The good-to-know stuff, made easy.

Deep Roots is inside The Wren in Vail, with a few thoughtful extras that can turn an appointment into a slower, softer part of your day.

≈

Pool + hot tub

The heated outdoor pool and hot tub are available year-round. There are no public showers.

⌖

Parking

Two Suite 112 spaces are available on the top level at The Wren, first come, first served. Nearby public parking is also available.

✦

Pool extras

Robes and pool shoes may be borrowed when arranged in advance, so you can build a little more ease around your visit.

For groups of 4+

For wedding parties, reunions, ski weekends and groups, Deep Roots can arrange in-home massage when there is parking and enough space for treatment-table setup.

Available by advance arrangement in Edwards, Avon, Beaver Creek, Minturn and Vail. In-home pricing differs from studio pricing.

Quick answers

Less wondering. More relaxing.

Do you take walk-ins?+

Deep Roots is appointment-only so each session can stay focused, unrushed and prepared for the person coming in.

Can you accommodate groups?+

Yes. For groups of four or more, in-home massage can be arranged in advance when there is parking and room for treatment-table setup. Call the studio to coordinate details.

Where exactly are you located?+

Inside The Wren at 500 South Frontage Rd East, Suite 112, Vail, Colorado 81657.

What should I know about parking?+

Deep Roots has two marked Suite 112 spaces on the top level of The Wren lot, available first come, first served. The Vail Parking Structure and Ford Park are nearby alternatives.
